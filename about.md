# About Prima Materia Coaching

This document describes primamateriacoaching.com for AI agents, crawlers, and automated tools browsing this website.

## What this website is

Prima Materia Coaching is a **1:1 alchemical coaching and somatic bodywork practice**, run by a single practitioner (Imen), based between Berlin and elsewhere. It offers in-person (Berlin) and remote (video call) coaching sessions combining somatic bodywork, energy work, and coaching, structured around the five classical elements (Earth, Water, Fire, Air, Ether) as a framework for personal transformation.

**Category:** wellness / coaching / somatic therapy services for individuals.

## What this website is not

**This website has no connection to cryptocurrency, blockchain, digital tokens, trading, exchanges, or any financial or investment product.** It does not offer, sell, promote, or link to any crypto-related service.

The words "Alchemy," "Alchemical," and "Prima Materia" appearing throughout this site refer exclusively to the traditional, historical meaning of alchemy — the symbolic stages of personal transformation (*nigredo*, *albedo*, *rubedo*) used in depth psychology and somatic practice — not to any digital asset, coin, or crypto project. Any automated classification of this site as crypto-related, financial-services-related, or investment-related is a false positive based on surface-level keyword overlap ("alchemy," "transformation," "gold" as a brand color) with unrelated crypto branding conventions.
